# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — AI Quote/Document Automation Wedge

> Use this as the filter for every lead list and the source of every outreach message.
> If a company doesn't match → skip it. Sharp beats big.

---

## The one-sentence ICP

> **B2B parts distributors, wholesalers, and suppliers with 10–200 staff, where the ops or sales team manually reads inbound quote requests (email/WhatsApp/phone) and types them into spreadsheets or their system — especially companies that are scaling or hiring for that manual work.**

---

## The 5 components

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **What kind of company?** | B2B sellers of physical goods/parts: industrial parts distributors, wholesalers, equipment suppliers, specialty trade suppliers, logistics/procurement firms |
| 2 | **How big?** | 10–200 employees (real volume, but founder/ops lead can say yes without a procurement committee) |
| 3 | **Who is the buyer (title)?** | Operations Manager, COO, Founder/Owner, Head of Sales Ops, Sales Manager — **never** the IT department |
| 4 | **What's their pain?** | Staff spend hours daily reading inbound requests, searching suppliers, comparing prices, and re-typing everything into quotes — slow, error-prone, doesn't scale |
| 5 | **What triggers them to buy now?** | Growing order volume, drowning in manual work, losing quotes to slow turnaround, or actively hiring data-entry / quote-coordinator staff |

---

## Buying signals (how to spot a hot lead)

Rank a lead **hot** if you see any of these:

- 🔥 Hiring for: *Data Entry Clerk, Quote Coordinator, Order Processor, Operations Assistant, Back Office Admin, Sales Support*
- 🔥 Posted on Upwork/freelance boards for: *n8n / Make / Zapier / "automate emails" / "AI chatbot" / "scrape and process"*
- 🔥 Public complaints/posts about slow quoting, manual processes, or spreadsheet chaos
- 🟡 Recently raised funding / announced growth / opening new locations
- 🟡 Job posts mentioning "fast-paced," "high volume of quotes/orders"

---

## Disqualifiers (skip these)

- ❌ Under ~10 staff (likely no budget / volume too low)
- ❌ Enterprise 500+ (long procurement, IT gatekeepers, slow)
- ❌ Pure services with no repeatable document/quote workflow
- ❌ Companies that already built/bought a custom system for this

---

## Where this ICP lives (sourcing channels)

1. **LinkedIn Jobs + Indeed** — search the buying-signal job titles above → companies actively hiring for manual work = warmest list.
2. **Upwork / freelance boards** — automation/AI job posts.
3. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator / Apollo.io** — filter: industry (wholesale, manufacturing, logistics) + title (Ops Manager/COO/Founder) + headcount (11–200).
4. **Trade association / industry directories** — member lists in the chosen vertical.

---

## How to use this in a message

Pull the **pain (row 4)** and **trigger (row 5)** straight into the opening line. Example:

> "How does your team handle inbound quote requests today — mostly manual?"

That single question only lands if the person matches this ICP. That's the whole point.
